- Transfer opponent units (bench + field) onto a host arena board at `Phase.Combat` start.
- Return transferred units at combat end (enter `Phase.Rewards` / next `Phase.Planning`).
- Preserve/restore original `(BoardActor, FBrawlGridCoord)` for each transferred unit.
